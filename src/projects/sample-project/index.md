The brief called for a desk organisation system that could adapt to different working styles and spaces. Starting from observations of how people actually use their desks, the design centres on a simple bent-steel frame that accepts interchangeable cork and steel modules.

The modular approach means users can configure the system for their specific needs — whether that's holding pens and tools, managing cables, or displaying reference materials. Each module clips into the frame without tools.

Material choices were driven by durability and tactile quality: powder-coated steel for structure, natural cork for surfaces that contact objects.
